Privacy Policy

Application: coreconf-m2m  |  Last updated: June 1, 2026

Summary: This application does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

1. Overview

coreconf-m2m is an educational and research tool demonstrating the CORECONF protocol stack (CoAP + CBOR + YANG SIDs) for IoT machine-to-machine communication, developed at IMT Atlantique as part of the SCHC Chair research program.

2. Data Collection

This application does not collect any personal data. Specifically:

  • No account or login is required.
  • No name, email address, or contact information is collected.
  • No location data is accessed or stored.
  • No usage analytics or crash reports are sent to any server.
  • No advertising identifiers are used.

3. Network Access

The application may communicate with CoAP/CORECONF demonstration servers (e.g. coap://h-schc.openschc.net) solely for the purpose of illustrating protocol behavior. No personal data is included in these exchanges. All communication uses standard IoT protocols (CoAP, CBOR) and contains only technical demonstration payloads.

4. Third-Party Services

This application does not integrate any third-party SDK, advertising network, analytics service, or social media plugin.

5. Data Storage

Any data stored locally on the device (e.g. configuration parameters) remains on the device and is never transmitted externally.

6. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for researchers, engineers, and students. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from minors.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised date. Given the nature of the application, no material changes are anticipated.
